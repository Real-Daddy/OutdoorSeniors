Overview

At OutdoorSeniors, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We keep things simple: your data is yours, and we treat it with care.

Information We Collect

When you reach out to us, we may collect:

  • ✓ Your name and contact details
  • ✓ The care you need (type of care, duration, location)
  • ✓ Preferences you share during caregiver matching
  • ✓ Feedback and service history

Like most websites, ours also collects general usage information automatically, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. This does not identify you by name or phone number.

How We Use Your Information

  • ✓ To match you with the right verified caregiver
  • ✓ To manage your booking and placement
  • ✓ To follow up on service quality
  • ✓ To improve our website and services

Your Contact Details Stay With Us

We never sell, rent or share your name, phone number, address or care details with anyone so that they can market to you or contact you. Ever.

Only our own team and the caregiver placed with you see them, and the caregiver receives only what they need to do the job. We may disclose information only where the law requires it.

Cookies and Advertising

We use standard analytics and advertising tools to understand how our website is used and to show relevant ads. These tools never receive your name, phone number or address. You can block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Keeping Your Data Safe

Your information is stored securely with reputable service providers, and only authorised members of our team can access it. Conversations on WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. To make such a request, simply contact us on WhatsApp.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to check back periodically.

Questions about your data?
