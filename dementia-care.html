The Process

How it works

  1. Tell us the stage

    Early, moderate or advanced — and which part of the day is hardest.

  2. We match on temperament

    For dementia this matters more than experience. Patience is the qualification.

  3. Overlap on day one

    The caretaker meets your parent while a family member is present, so it isn’t a stranger arriving alone.

  4. Adjust as it changes

    Dementia progresses. We review the arrangement as the needs shift.

Good to know

Questions families ask

My mother gets aggressive. Will they cope?

Tell us upfront and in detail. Aggression is common in mid-stage dementia, and we’d far rather match correctly than have a placement collapse in week one.

Will she even accept a stranger?

Often not at first. That’s why we plan for an overlap period with the family present rather than pretending the handover will be smooth.

Does it have to be the same person every day?

For dementia, yes — it’s essential. A new face daily makes everything worse, so continuity is non-negotiable here.

How do you verify your caretakers and nurses?

Everyone we place goes through document verification, reference checks and police verification before they are placed with you. For nurses we additionally verify the qualification against their nursing registration.

How does pricing work?

It depends on the hours, the level of care, and whether the role needs a nurse or a caretaker. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we’ll share exact pricing — all-inclusive, GST included, no hidden costs.

What if the person isn’t the right fit?

Tell us and we’ll replace them at no extra cost. It usually happens in the first week if it happens at all, and it isn’t a problem — getting the match right matters more than getting it right first time.
