Why OutdoorSeniors

Built by People Who Needed This Too

We started OutdoorSeniors after struggling to find reliable care for our own parents. Here is what we do differently.

🔍

Verified Before Placement

Document checks, references and police verification happen before anyone enters your home. Nurses’ qualifications are verified against their registration.

🎯

Matched, Not Just Assigned

We match on the things that actually decide whether it works — language, temperament, and experience with your specific situation.

🔄

Free Replacement

If the match is not right, we replace at no extra cost. It usually happens in the first week if it happens at all, and it isn’t a problem.

💬

Honest About Fit

If your situation needs a nurse rather than a caretaker, or a hospital rather than home care, we’ll say so. Even when it costs us the booking.

💰

Clear Pricing, Upfront

Pricing depends on hours and level of care, so we share it once we understand what you need — all-inclusive, GST included, no surprises later.

🤝

A Real Person on WhatsApp

From your first message through to renewals, you’re talking to someone who knows your case — not a ticket number.
